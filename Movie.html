My Movie List By Johnson Tomson

Best Movies of all times

The Shawshank Redemption (1994)

I like how it handles hope, patience, friendship, and resilience without feeling forced.

The Godfather (1972)

Incredible character development, especially Michael Corleone's gradual transformation.

Interstellar (2014)

I love the mix of science, huge cosmic ideas, emotion, sacrifice, and the father-daughter transformation

The Dark Knight (2008 )

The Joker-Batman conflict is more philosophical than normal superhero story, and Health Ledger is unforgettable.

Gladiator (2000)

Powerful revenge story, memory dialogue, great music, and Maximus is an extremely compelling character.

Parasite (2019)

Brilliant because it can be funny, uncomfortable, suspenseful, and tragic while exploring class inequality.

The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (2003)

Epic scale, friendship, sacrifice, courage, and one of the most satisfying conclusions to a trilogy.

12 Angry Men (1957)

Almost the entire film is people talking in one room, yet it becomes intensely gripping through reasoning, prejudice, and persuasion.

Good Will Hunting (1997)

What stands out to me is that underneath the intelligence and humour, its really about trauma, friendship, vulnerability, and deciding what to do with your potential.
